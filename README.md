Round 3: a synthesis front page for Wednesday 18 November 2026 — the breadth of round 1 with the legibility discipline of round 2. Six big picture-led stories carry the day: the Kerch bridge span falling into the sea, the Gulf war widening, Nvidia's record quarter, Anthropic pricing its IPO, the Quad meeting in Manila, and Gaza facing winter. Below them a numbered world-map strip pins each story to its place, and 26 one-line briefs across regions and beats fill out the day. Thirty-two stories in all, every headline kept at 28px or larger and every brief at 15px on a single line, so the page stays readable at thumbnail size. The six photographs and the map were generated for this entry and carry no baked-in text; all wording is drawn on top. The program copies the vendored image to /output/entry.png byte for byte, so both runs write identical bytes with no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
